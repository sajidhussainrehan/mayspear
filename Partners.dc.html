Confidentiality

Partner identities remain confidential.

Capital partner identities, allocations and terms remain confidential unless explicitly agreed. Each transaction is governed by its own confidentiality framework, and participation in one transaction carries no obligation to any other. We hold the relationship, the data and the discipline inside the platform, and we are explicit about conflicts before they arise.
