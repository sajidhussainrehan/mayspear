GAP 01

The sub-$50M mid-market

$5-50MDeals below the megafund floor
The gap

A $20 billion fund earns nothing meaningful on a $15 million deal, the work is identical to a $1 billion deal but the return barely registers. So the giants set a floor, typically around $50 million, and simply will not look below it. Below that line sits the overwhelming majority of real businesses.

How we fill it

This band is our core. We transact from $5 million with full institutional structuring and the speed of a principal, and we scale to $50 billion when a single situation warrants it. We are large enough to be credible and small enough to care about a $10 million ticket.

GAP 02

Founder succession

$10T+Founder value, no succession plan
The gap

A founding generation built across decades is now retiring, holding trillions in enterprise value with no successor. Banks will not lend through a change of control. Large sponsors want bigger platforms. The result is the largest ownership transfer in history with almost no capital designed to serve it.

How we fill it

We acquire and recapitalise founder-owned businesses, back the next management team, and structure liquidity for the owner, preserving the company, the jobs and the legacy. It is a vast, fragmented opportunity that sits exactly in our size band.

GAP 03

Africa & emerging markets

$80B+Africa's annual trade-finance gap
The gap

Development banks and global lenders leave an enormous gap across Africa and emerging markets, by some estimates over $80 billion a year in African trade finance alone, plus an infrastructure deficit running into the hundreds of billions. Risk is mispriced as binary; good cash flows go unbanked.

How we fill it

With offices in Lagos, Dubai, Singapore and São Paulo and origination across the corridors, we structure against offtake, receivables and real assets, turning perceived risk into secured, fundable transactions. Emerging-market capability is not aspiration for us; it is operational infrastructure.

GAP 04

Mid-tier mining & commodities

Mid-tierThe band majors no longer bank
The gap

Resource majors and commodity banks fund only tier-one assets and the largest names. The growth-stage miner, the mid-tier producer, the regional trader, all sit in a structural funding vacuum, despite hard assets, reserves and contracted offtake behind them.

How we fill it

We finance against the commodity itself: prepayment, streaming, royalty, borrowing-base and pre-export structures. We take asset and commodity risk that conventional lenders cannot underwrite, in one of the hottest under-banked pools in global capital.

GAP 05

DIP & rescue finance

DaysThe timeframe distress allows
The gap

When a company hits a liquidity wall or enters a court process, capital must arrive in days, secured on priority claims, against a credible plan. Mega-funds move on committee cycles measured in months; banks have already exited. Lower-mid-market distress is left to fend for itself.

How we fill it

We provide debtor-in-possession, bridge and rescue finance with the conviction to commit principal capital inside the window the situation allows. We underwrite the collateral and the turnaround, and we fund when others are still scheduling the first meeting.

GAP 06

Platform deal flow

$1-50MVolume others cannot hold
The gap

Large trading and financial platforms originate enormous volumes of smaller transactions they cannot place on their own balance sheet. The flow is real and creditworthy, but the appetite to hold it is not there, so it is pushed away or left unfunded.

How we fill it

We are the capital arm those platforms call. We fund or arrange the deal flow they cannot hold, keep their client relationship intact, and provide certainty of capital at speed. One conversation can unlock a recurring pipeline.

GAP 07

Energy and the transition

TrillionsAnnual transition funding need
The gap

Power, grid, storage, transition and critical mineral projects are capital intensive and technically complex. Banks cap their exposure and generalist funds lack the conviction to underwrite the asset.

How we fill it

We structure debt, equity and hybrid capital for energy and transition assets, verify them with specialists on the ground, and move at the pace these projects demand.

GAP 09

Large deals, on the radar

Co-investAcross the line, not only below it
