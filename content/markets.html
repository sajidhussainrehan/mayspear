The frontier edge

Emerging markets aren't a footnote to the thesis, they are it.

The sharpest mispricing of risk in global capital sits in Africa, the Gulf, Latin America and emerging Asia, vast trade-finance gaps, an infrastructure deficit measured in hundreds of billions a year, and resource and energy systems starved of structured capital. The largest firms treat these markets as binary risk and stay away.

We treat them as our home advantage. With partners in Lagos, Dubai, Singapore and São Paulo, we originate locally, structure against real cash flow and assets, and bring global capital into transactions others cannot see, let alone underwrite.
