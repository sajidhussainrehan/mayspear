Mayspear deploys capital in partnership with institutional and private capital providers on a transaction-by-transaction basis. Partners engage at the level of the individual transaction, with full structural clarity, never through a blind pool. We invest our own capital alongside, in every transaction we originate.

The model

Every deal is structured so our interests line up with yours.

Each opportunity is presented on its own terms. Partners select the transactions and the position in the capital structure that fit their mandate, with Mayspear invested and accountable alongside.

◆Co-investment

Direct co-investment

Invest directly alongside Mayspear in a specific transaction, on aligned terms and at a defined position in the capital structure.

◆Syndication

Syndicated participation

Take an allocation within a club or syndicate that Mayspear structures, arranges and leads.

◆Structured

Structured participation

Participate through a structured or tranched instrument calibrated to a specific risk and return profile.

◆Allocation

Deal-by-deal allocation

Review and select transactions individually. No blind pool, no lockup, no obligation beyond the deal taken.

Process flow

Origination to close, and back again.

01

Origination

Mayspear sources and originates the transaction across its corridors and channels.

02

Structuring & underwriting

The transaction is underwritten loss-first and structured to an institutional standard.

03

Partner allocation

The opportunity is presented to relevant partners for transaction-level participation.

04

Execution & close

Mayspear leads documentation, coordination and closing, invested alongside.

05

Reporting & recycling

Servicing, monitoring and reporting through Radnor, then capital is recycled into the next.

Confidentiality

Partner identities remain confidential.

Capital partner identities, allocations and terms remain confidential unless explicitly agreed. Each transaction is governed by its own confidentiality framework, and participation in one transaction carries no obligation to any other. We hold the relationship, the data and the discipline inside the platform, and we are explicit about conflicts before they arise.
