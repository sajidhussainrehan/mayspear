The thesis

We are quick to commit, and careful with everything after.

Our core mandate is large, complex transactions. Corporate finance, private credit, infrastructure, energy and special situations from $50M to $5BN, led as principal and structured alongside the largest funds. This is where capital structure design, speed, and execution certainty decide the outcome, and it is where Mayspear operates first.

We also refuse to leave good value behind. A dedicated desk, run from Ireland, originates and places smaller tickets in energy, industrial and logistics assets, so a strong transaction below our core threshold still finds capital. Scale is our mandate, and the desk ensures disciplined capital still reaches sound opportunities below it.
