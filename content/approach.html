01 / The thesis

We were built for the band the giants can't reach.

Every structural shift of the last decade, fund consolidation, bank retreat, the retirement of a founding generation, the under-banking of emerging markets, has opened a band of profitable, fundable transactions that the largest institutions physically cannot reach.

These are not bad deals. They are good deals in the wrong size, the wrong place, or the wrong moment for a $20 billion fund. Too small to move the needle for a mega-fund, too complex for a bank's credit committee, too fast or too far from a head office. This is the band Mayspear owns, as principal, on our own balance sheet, and alongside the largest funds where scale demands it.

03 / The criteria

The investment box: what qualifies for capital.

The thesis and the capabilities only matter inside defined parameters. These are the sectors, structures, sizing and boundaries that determine whether a transaction is one we can lead.

Ticket size

$1M to $5BN. A $50M–$5BN core deployed as principal and alongside the largest funds; a dedicated desk extends the platform down to $1M.

Position in the structure

Senior secured, unitranche and stretch senior, mezzanine and second lien, structured and preferred equity, and outright control.

Sectors

The real economy: corporate credit, private equity, special situations, energy and minerals, infrastructure, trade and asset-backed.

Geographies

Developed and emerging corridors where we have people on the ground, New York, London, Lagos, Dubai, Singapore and São Paulo.

Stage

Performing, transitional, stressed and distressed. Complexity and dislocation are where we are sharpest, not where we stop.

Security

Enforceable collateral, a defined cash-flow waterfall and active monitoring. We underwrite the asset, not the label.

Outside the box

Blind-pool commitments, pure early-stage venture, retail or consumer-direct products, and any sanctioned counterparty or jurisdiction. We structure jurisdiction risk; we do not accept it blind.

04 / The desk

We refuse to leave good value below $50M.

Our core mandate is large, complex transactions from $50M to $5BN. But structural value does not stop at a threshold. A dedicated desk, run from Ireland, originates and places smaller transactions from $1M, energy, industrial and logistics assets that need capital, placement or coordination, with the same structuring discipline applied to the institutional book.
