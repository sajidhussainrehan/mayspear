SECTION 01

We are the infrastructure beneath private capital.

Mayspear Global originates, structures and distributes capital across constrained cash flows in the real economy. We are the connective infrastructure between real economy financing demand and global institutional liquidity, committing our own capital as principal and moving at a speed conventional institutions cannot match. We invest at institutional scale, as principal and alongside the largest funds, and no situation is too complex for us to structure.

Scale

Institutional cheques from $20M to $5BN, led as principal and alongside the largest funds.

Speed

One decision line and our own co-invested capital let us commit in days, not quarters.

Structure

The whole capital stack, debt, equity and hybrid, engineered from capital we co-invest in every transaction.

Speed is the asset

In constrained situations, certainty of capital and certainty of timing decide the outcome. We co-invest in every transaction, our mandate is pre-cleared, and the people who structure a transaction are the people who approve it. No committee queue, no external credit cycle, no handoff. We commit in days, where others take months.

What we are

A capital architecture layer, not a single product. We do not operate as a lender alone, nor as a closed end fund. We turn constrained real economy cash flows into investable institutional product, and allocate global liquidity into them, starting from the constraint, never the product.
